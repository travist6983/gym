About Hardworx

Our Vision / Mission

HardWorx is designed for people to maximize their workout in a timely manner, while enhancing their potential within themselves. Time is Money & we focus on having an environment where our members are no longer waiting for equipment & are having to extend their workouts longer than originally wanted. We emphasize the cleanliness of our Gym, Customer Service, & Strong Community to ensure you have the best experience. We cap our membership at 500 members to ensure there are no crowds.

Facilities

At HardWorx, we offer a variety of State-Of-The-Art Equipment. This includes Cardio Machines, Pin-Stack and Plate Loaded, Arsenal, Atlantis, PowerLift, Hammer Strength, Cybex, and Panatta Machines, along with a large selection of Free Weights to meet the wants and needs for all fitness backgrounds.

Workout Plans & Programs

At HardWorx Gym, we take pride in being the best health and wellness home for individuals of all fitness levels. One of the biggest concerns most people have when they step foot in the gym is knowing what exercises to do, what order to do them in, and how many sets and repetitions are necessary to reach the desired goals. Here at HardWorx, we strive to eliminate that issue all together. Through the use of the HardWorx App, members will have a platform to view their custom workouts, log their weights and repetitions to keep track of progress, along with access to an extensive database of videos of all the different exercises you will complete on a day to day basis.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Feel free to reach out to us by phone at (586) 262-4772 or by email at Manager.shelby23@hardworxgym.com if you cannot find an answer to your question.

    What are your hours of operation?

    We are open 24/7 for HardWorx Members. If you would like to see our Staff Hours, please click the "About Us" Tab.

    What type of equipment do you offer?

    We offer cardio equipment, pin, and plate loaded machines. We also offer a variety of Free Weights. We offer Equipment from Multiple Different Companies: True Fitness, Cybex, Hammer Strength, Arsenal, Muscle D, Panatta & Atlantis Equipment.

    Do you have locker rooms and showers?

    We have a large space of lockers for all members. We have 6 private restrooms and 3 showers.

    What is the guest policy?

    Members may bring Guests for Visits and Trials during STAFFED HOURS ONLY. There are NO Guests allowed in the Facility under any circumstances during Non-Staffed Hours.
